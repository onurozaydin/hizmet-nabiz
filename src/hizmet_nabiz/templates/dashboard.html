How to read this dashboard

  • Delay index compares each request with the typical duration for the same agency and complaint type; 1.00 is peer-expected.
  • High-delay EB shrinks noisy board rates toward the city average using an empirical-Bayes prior.
  • Excess hours are observed hours above peer medians. They are a workload signal, not guaranteed savings.
  • The capacity scenario assumes a 25% reduction in observed excess delay for reviewed cases; it is explicitly hypothetical and non-causal.
Source: NYC OpenData / NYC311 dataset erm2-nwe9. Exact query, extract hash, KPI contract, quality report and limitations are included in the repository.
